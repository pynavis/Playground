Project > Quantities

Zone Takeoff

Measures objects by zone, from grid levels, rooms or areas, grid bays or square tiles, and by group. Exports an Excel pivot with a sheet per zone.

Tab and panelProject > Quantities
KindButton
Live whenA model is open

Status

Untested Never executed inside Navisworks. Passed the headless checks only.

Related asks to confirm

No quote is on record yet. These threads look related, but a person still has to open and read them.

  • https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/navisworks-forum/automation-of-quantity-takeoff-by-search-sets/td-p/12145874

Events it listens to

None. It runs only when you click it.

Files

Paths are relative to the Playground.extension folder.

  • Project.tab/Quantities.panel/Zone_Takeoff.pushbutton/bundle.yaml
  • Project.tab/Quantities.panel/Zone_Takeoff.pushbutton/icon.dark.png
  • Project.tab/Quantities.panel/Zone_Takeoff.pushbutton/icon.png
  • Project.tab/Quantities.panel/Zone_Takeoff.pushbutton/icon.small.dark.png
  • Project.tab/Quantities.panel/Zone_Takeoff.pushbutton/icon.small.png
  • Project.tab/Quantities.panel/Zone_Takeoff.pushbutton/script.py

Install

  1. Only this tool. In Navisworks, click pyNavis > Playground, search for Zone Takeoff, tick it and click Install.
  2. The whole extension. Download Playground.extension.zip, unzip it, put the Playground.extension folder in %APPDATA%\pyNavis\extensions\, and click pyNavis > Reload. Zone Takeoff is on the Project tab, Quantities panel.

You need pyNavis 1.3.0 or later.
